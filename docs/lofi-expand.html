02

Your seven notes, and what each one changed

Button overlap
Moved under the copy. It was floating over the stack’s lower right, which is exactly where the receded cards’ depth strip rides through. Under the text it is constant and never collides.
Lost folder shape
Two causes. The sheet reused the 1600 × 1100 viewBox with preserveAspectRatio="none", so at screen aspect every radius and the tab height stretched out of true. It now builds its path in real pixel space, recomputed each frame. And a tab widened to full width is a header bar, not a tab.
The expanded view
It is one folder carrying all three tabs. You opened the stack and got a single document whose tabs are its sections. During the expand the sibling tabs grow from nothing at their own slots, so the folder visibly sprouts them.
Tabs, not flipping
They scroll to sections, and the active tab now follows your scroll position. The last build already scrolled, but nothing reflected where you were, so it still read as a flip. Three tabs on one body is what makes them read as sections of one thing.
The text moves
The number, title and tagline fly from their homepage position into the masthead on the same 600ms curve as the frame, and fly back on close.
Less text up top
The masthead is the flown-in identity plus a metadata row, and the four statements are a 2 × 2 hairline grid instead of a vertical stack. Work starts within one screen.
The balance
Identity → metadata → four statements → three sections, each opening on one large lead artifact with supporting stills beneath. First pass; the ratio is the thing to judge.
