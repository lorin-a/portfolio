Client Talk
	•	Documentation of decisions that were made, how things happened throughout the birth process
	•	700 women die each year from pregnancy-related complications in USA, 70% of these are near misses
	•	80% are preventable
	•	65% occur after delivery
	•	1 in 3 occur after discharge
	•	Black women are 3x more likely to die from pregnancy-related causes than white women
	•	USA is leading in pregnancy deaths
	•	Depression Suicide + interpersonal violence are leading causes of post-birth deaths
	•	Postpartum Period = 6-10 weeks most vulnerable (preeclampsia, hemorrhage) - 1 year (depression, overdose, suicide)
	•	Postpartum is a period of time, postpartum depression is something that can happen (15% feel this)
	•	Why? delays in diagnosis/effective treatment, knowledge of warning signs, now knowing when to get help, limited time with healthcare providers
	•	Myana: Track, Engage, Triage, Support
	•	Track: symptoms of physical and emotional
	•	Triage: symptoms to the appropriate level of care, letting them know when to seek help
	•	Engage: people in postpartum care, how to care for yourself
	•	Support: personalized support
	•	Moms using it said they needed to also have some baby information, 80% mom 20% baby first 6 weeks and then shift to 20% mom 80% baby toward 1 year
	•	They have a pregnancy app as well. People showing depression symptoms were using the app more
	•	Look @ Myana documentation
	•	Open ended journaling was used, learned what language they use to describe their experience
