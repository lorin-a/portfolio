## ACT I · SENSE

### 01 · The tension
- **HEADING (proposed):** Birth gets overshadowed by the newborn's needs.
- **Your words:** “Birthing parents lack systems of support before, during, and after the experience
  of giving birth, which is often overshadowed by the needs of a newborn.”

### 02 · What we heard
- **HEADING (proposed):** Simple beats comprehensive: postpartum brain fog needs less, not more.
- **Your words:** “We interviewed 7 parents to learn about their birth experiences, and many reported
  that their experiences were traumatic and that they lacked tools to process them.” … “We created
  lo-fi wireframes that attempted to offer as many tools as possible but through feedback we learned
  that **simple is better for postpartum brain fog.**”
- **ASSET:** a parent quote, set large. `[LORIN TO WRITE — one real interview quote, if you have one you can use]`

### 03 · How I worked
- **HEADING (proposed):** Six weeks, from client brief to client presentation.
- **Your words (the phases):** Research I · Research II · Ideation · Interviews · Iteration ·
  Presentation (1 week each).
- **ASSET:** TIMELINE (proportional, 6 phases) or PROCESS STEPPER.

## ACT II · WEAVE

### 04 · The concept
- **HEADING (proposed):** One home that holds both documenting and reflecting.
- **Your words:** “We initially created a simple user flow, dividing the app into two primary
  functions: Document and Reflect. However, after receiving parent feedback, we redesigned the home
  page to integrate both functions. This reduced barriers to entry and allowed users to immediately
  engage based on their needs in the moment.”
- **ASSET:** BEFORE / AFTER — two separate functions → one integrated home. (Your clearest design decision.)

### 05 · Care Pod
- **HEADING (proposed):** Birth is held by a circle, so the app invited one in.
- **Your words:** “Research revealed users want to collaborate with loved ones, leading to the Care
  Pod feature — a curated inner circle that shares supportive messages and receives birth updates.”
- **ASSET:** DEVICE FRAME (the Care Pod flow).

### 06 · Birth Story Book
- **HEADING (proposed):** A keepsake, because people fear losing the memory.
- **Your words:** “Because users don't fully trust that digital memories will be preserved
  long-term, the Birth Story Book transforms entries into a physical keepsake via free PDF or
  affordable printed booklet.”
- **ASSET:** PHOTOGRAPH (the printed booklet) + the quote: “It would be tragic to lose those moments
  if the app disappeared someday.”

### 07 · The copy pivot (your call as the writer)
- **HEADING (proposed):** I rewrote the voice from trauma-first to community-first.
- **Your words:** “Our original approach to the copy was highly trauma-informed, but feedback
  revealed the language to be leading toward assumptions of a negative experience. While negative
  experiences are reflected in the data, we decided to take a more holistic, user-driven approach to
  the content with an emphasis on connecting with community.”
- **ASSET:** BEFORE / AFTER (copy) — trauma-informed line → holistic line. *This is a strong
  researcher-strategist beat; it shows judgment, not just screens.*

### 08 · Brand identity
- **HEADING (proposed):** A calm interface for the wee hours and the harsh hospital light.
- **Your words:** “We wanted the interface to feel calming, knowing that this app would be used in
  wee hours of the morning or in harsh hospital light … organic and emotionally supportive —
  creating a safe space for difficult emotions and uplifting moments alike. We also … created a new
  gradient to match Myana's gradient style.”
- **ASSET:** brand board / DEVICE FRAME (the gradient, type, components).

## CLOSE

### What I'd do differently  *(your words — already written, no gap)*
> “If I could do this project again, I'd begin by simplifying. Identifying the core need early would
> have helped me stay focused, rather than trying to do too much at once. I also learned how
> important it is not to add anything to a wireframe that distracts from the main purpose or opens
> unnecessary avenues for feedback.”
